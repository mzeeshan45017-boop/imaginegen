Information we collect

  • Account information: name and email address if you sign in.
  • Prompts and generated images: the text you submit and the images we return, used to provide the service and, unless you opt out, to improve moderation and quality.
  • Usage data: IP address, device/browser type, and generation counts, used for rate limiting and abuse prevention.
  • Cookies / local storage: used to remember your generation count and sign-in state on this device.

How we use information

  • To generate images and deliver the service you request.
  • To enforce free-tier limits and prevent abuse.
  • To detect and block prohibited content (see our Content Policy).
  • To show relevant advertising through third-party ad networks (e.g. Google AdSense), which may set their own cookies. See Google's guidance on how it uses data from sites using its services.

Sharing

We share prompts with our AI generation provider (Google's Gemini API) solely to produce your images. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can request deletion of your account data by contacting us. You can clear local generation history by clearing your browser's site data.
