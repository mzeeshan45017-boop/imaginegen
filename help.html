Common questions

How many images can I generate for free?

5 images without signing in, then up to 100 images per day once you sign in with your name and email — no credit card required.

Is there a watermark?

No. All images, free or paid, are delivered without a watermark.

What can I use the images for?

Personal and commercial use, as long as your prompt and use follow our Content Policy.

My image didn't generate — what happened?

This can happen if the prompt was blocked by our content filter, if you've hit your daily limit, or if there was a temporary server issue. Try rephrasing your prompt or trying again in a few minutes.
