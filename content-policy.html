Prohibited content

You may not use ImagineGen to generate, and we will remove and may report:

  • Sexual content involving minors, in any form.
  • Non-consensual sexual or intimate imagery of real people, including "deepfakes."
  • Content that promotes violence, terrorism, or hatred against a person or group.
  • Instructions for creating weapons, explosives, or other dangerous items.
  • Content that infringes another person's copyright or trademark, or closely imitates a living artist's distinctive style in a way intended to pass off as their work.
  • Defamatory or misleading depictions of real, identifiable people (e.g. fake news imagery).

How we moderate

Prompts are screened before generation and images are screened after generation using automated filters. Accounts that repeatedly attempt to generate prohibited content may be suspended. If you believe a piece of content was blocked in error, contact us.
